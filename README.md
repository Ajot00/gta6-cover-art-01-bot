Vice Press is a GTA VI-inspired cover art editor that turns a photo and caption into a multi-panel poster with bold lettering and a Vice City sunset or neon colour grade. Choose from six layouts and five grades, drag and zoom photos to crop and position them, fill panels with one or several images, and customize the title and tagline. Export your finished cover as a PNG at 1080×1080, 1080×1350, or 1920×1080. The app runs offline from its own files with `node serve.ts`.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```
